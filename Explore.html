Dive into an array of unique experiences that showcase the authentic charm and rich history of Cyprus. Explore beyond the usual tourist attractions and discover the hidden pearls that make this island so special.

Our Top Picks

  • Omodos Village
    Wander through the cobblestone streets of this picturesque village, renowned for its traditional architecture and local wineries.
  • Cave Cavo Greco
    Rugged peninsula & National park with cliffside hikes, a turquoise lagoon & a natural rock bridge. Explore the untouched beauty of this nature reserve, perfect for hiking and breathtaking coastal views.
  • Kato Drys Village
    Visit this quaint village known for its preserved stone houses and charming local crafts.
  • Blue Lagoon
    Crystal-clear turquoise waters and white sandy beaches, it's a paradise for sun-seekers and nature lovers. Enjoy the serene atmosphere, with fresh seafood restaurants and boat / jeep/ car trips to the Blue Lagoon.
  • Kykkos Monastery
    Discover the spiritual heart of Cyprus at this stunning monastery, home to beautiful frescoes and a rich history.
  • Troodos Mountains
    Experience the lush landscapes, traditional villages, and scenic drives in this mountainous region.

Family Favorite

Donkey Farm

The Donkeys and Traditions excursion is an interactive journey that satisfies all senses and entertains both adults and children. You will have the opportunity to see how the traditional Cypriot products such as: cheese anari and halloumi and olive pie are made, to follow a tour of the farm, to see one of the oldest olive trees in the Mediterranean and learn about donkeys' life. The tour ends with a free tasting of the farm's products, including donkey milk. The final part of the excursion includes a dinner, wine and lively entertainment with Cypriot dances and music.

We guarantee that for all family this day will be full of joy, excitement and delight!

Top Family Pick

Camel Park

Make a gift for yourself: an amazing adventure day in the Camel Park!

The park is a home for more than 100 animals, including ostriches, goats, deers, ponies, wallabies, lamas, alpacas, lemurs, turtles, fish, a lot of birds and our 60 camels, that each of them has its own name and character. It is the biggest camel park in Europe(!), achieving the 'Trip Advisor' Certificate of Excellence for the past 10 years.

The main attraction! Camel rides are a must for everyone seeking to try new experiences. There are kids area, plenty of other things to do other than riding or feeding the camels, ranging from our swimming pool to donkey and pony rides!

We guarantee a day full of joy, excitement and delight for all ages!

You fasten your harness, secure your headset, and run through your pre-flight checklist. Each item you tick off brings you closer to the moment you've been dreaming about. You clear the tower for takeoff, and with a steady hand, push the throttle forward.

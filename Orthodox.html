PROGRAM

Mount Athos

Day 1

Meeting at the airport of Thessaloniki. Transfer to the hotel in Ouranoupolis. Accommodation at the hotel (50 Euro supplement for 1 bed accommodation). Dinner in the tavern at your expense (20-30 euros).

Day 2

Together with the group, receive diamonetirion and buy ferry tickets. Departure to the Skete of St. Anne, climbing to the top and veneration of the miraculous icon of St. Anna and her incorrupt foot. Walking to the Monastery of St. Paul. Evening service, veneration of the shrines of the monastery.

Day 3

Including the Gifts of the Magi. Meal. Overnight in the monastery.
Liturgy and meal. Transfer by ferry to the Monastery of Dionysios. Veneration of the miraculous Akathist Icon (created by St. Luke), the right hand of John the Baptist and the relics of St. Paraskeva. Transfer to Grigoriat Monastery. Evening service, veneration of the shrines of the monastery. Meal. Overnight stay.

Day 4

Liturgy and meal. Departure by ferry to the port of Dafni. Transfer to the bus, transfer to Karyes (the capital of Athos), veneration of the icon "It is truly meet". Transfer to the skete of St. Andrew the First-Called. Worship of His incorruptible head. Trekking to Koutloumoumous Monastery, veneration of shrines. Transfer to the Monastery of Iviron. Evening service. Veneration of the Icon of the Mother of God Portaitissa (Iveron). Meal. Overnight stay.

Day 5

Liturgy and meal. Transfer to the Monastery of Philotheus. Accommodation in the monastery and a walk to the Caracal Monastery. Veneration of the shrines of the monastery. Return to the Monastery of Philotheus. Evening service, veneration of shrines. Meal. Overnight stay.

Day 6

Liturgy and meal. Transfer to Dochiar Monastery. Veneration of the miraculous icon "Quick to Hearken". Trekking to the Xenophon Monastery. Veneration of the shrines of the monastery. Transfer to the Monastery of St. Panteleimon. Evening service. Meal. Overnight stay.

Day 7

Liturgy and meal at the Monastery of St. Panteleimon. Veneration of the head of the Great Martyr Panteleimon and the shrines of the monastery. Departure to Ouranoupolis. Accommodation at the hotel. A meal at your own expense in the tavern.

Day 8

Breakfast at the hotel. Free time, buying souvenirs. Transfer to the airport.

The cost is 740 euros.

The price includes: Prior permission to visit Athos, meals in monasteries according to the monastic rule, escort around Athos, individual transfer for the group airport-Ouranoupolis-airport. Accommodation in the hotel in Ouranoupolis for the first and last night in a single, double or triple room. Ferry tickets Ouranoupolis-Athos-Ouranoupolis.

The price of the tour does not include: air travel, insurance, visa, stamp duty (25 euros for obtaining a diamonetiron), state tax, which is paid upon check-in (3 euros per room). Transfers by ferries and buses within Athos (about 40-50 euros). Personal expenses. Note: Overnight accommodation in monastery hotels on Mount Athos does not imply specific accommodation. Overnight stays in the monasteries indicated in the program are not guaranteed! If the monastery is unable to accept the group for the night on the date specified in the program, the group will be accommodated in another monastery.

REPRESENTS A FOREIGN PILGRIMAGES
GEORGIA
7 DAYS

You will visit: Tbilisi. Sioni Cathedral: Apostle Thomas (head),
cross of St. Equal-to-the-Apostles Nina.
Patriarchal Holy Trinity Cathedral (Sameba).

Mtskheta. Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (4th century), under the lintel of which the Tunic of the Lord is placed.

Kakheti region, Bodbe Monastery of Saint Nina, plunge into the spring.
Sighnaghi, the city of love, the Alazani Valley.

Borjomi Resort. Monastery of St. George in Chitakhevi ("Green Monastery"). Monastery of St. Seraphim of Sarov. Monastery of St. George the Victorious 7th century. Borjomi water sources in Borjomi Park.

Warm sulfur baths in the open air in the Borjomi forest.

Kazbegi: Ananuri Temple 16th century place. Mount Kazbek (5000 m). High-mountain temple Tsminda Sameba - Holy Trinity (12th century) in Gergeti. Miraculous icon of the Gergeti Mother of God, place of appearances of Mother of God.

IKALTO MONASTERY. Medieval capital of Kakheti.

Walk through old Tbilisi. City market, dry bridge (souvenirs).

This is only a part of the list of what you will see :)

Georgian feast (gastronomic tour).

The price: 530 € per person.

REPRESENTS A FOREIGN PILGRIMAGES
ALBANIA – ITALY – NORTH MACEDONIA
5 days
VISITING THE RELICS OF
ST. NICHOLAS THE WONDERWORKER

TIRANA, Albania
The relics of the holy Tsar Vladimir - Elbasan.
Durres, Albania. Boarding the ferry: Bari, Italy. Overnight on board.

BARI, Italy
The relics of St. Nicholas.
Leisure, tour of the city of Bari. Evening service at the relics of St. Nicholas.

NORTH MACEDONIA
Tour the famous springs of Bilyan and visit the exceptional archaeological museum of the bay of bones on the shores of the lake.
Accommodation in the hotel complex of the monastery of St. Naum, located on the shore of Lake Ohrid, veneration of the relics of St. Naum.
Divine Liturgy at the relics of St. Naum of Ohrid.

Ohrid, North Macedonia. Accommodation at the hotel. Excursion to the old town of Ohrid, St. Sophia Church, St. John Kane Church, St. Clement Church, Church of the Blessed Virgin Mary Peribleptos, free time.

Divine Liturgy. Breakfast. Walk on Lake Ohrid. Visit to the monastery in Struga - the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary and the hermitage of St. Athanasius the Great. Visit the Orthodox village of Vevchany, where there are beautiful springs.
The surprise of this trip will be a Macedonian evening with a traditional dinner and music.

Breakfast. Free time, visiting workshops for the production of a unique Ohrid pearl and famous Macedonian products. Transfer to Tirana. Fligth Tirana – Larnaca.

The cost is 550 euros.

The price includes:
Accommodation in a 3* hotel with breakfast
Transfers
Ferry tickets: Durres – Bari – Durres
Orthodox guide

The price of the tour does not include: air travel, insurance, visa (if needed), personal expenses.
